# Crunch & Munch E-Commerce

Create a modern, visually appealing e-commerce website for a fictional snacks brand called “Crunch & Munch”.

Brand concept:

Crunch & Munch is a fun, youthful snack brand offering delicious, affordable, and high-quality snacks for students, young professionals, and families.

Website style:

- Modern, vibrant, playful, and premium

- Use warm snack-inspired colors such as orange, yellow, cream, and dark brown

- Clean white/cream backgrounds with bold colorful accents

- Rounded cards, soft shadows, attractive product photography, and smooth animations

- Use bold, friendly typography

- Make the website look appetizing and Instagram-friendly

- Fully responsive for desktop, tablet, and mobile

Homepage sections:

1. Hero Section

   - Large high-quality image of assorted snacks

   - Headline: “Crunch. Munch. Repeat.”

   - Subheading: “Your favourite snacks, made for every craving.”

   - CTA buttons: “Shop Now” and “Explore Snacks”

2. Featured Categories

   - Chips

   - Nachos

   - Popcorn

   - Cookies

   - Namkeen

   - Healthy Snacks

3. Best Sellers

   Display attractive product cards with:

   - Product image

   - Product name

   - Short description

   - Price

   - Rating

   - “Add to Cart” button

4. Why Choose Us?

   - Fresh & Crispy

   - Quality Ingredients

   - Affordable Prices

   - Fast Delivery

5. Special Offer Banner

   - “Get 20% OFF on your first order”

   - CTA: “Grab the Deal”

6. Customer Reviews

   Add 3–4 realistic customer testimonials with star ratings.

7. Instagram-style Gallery

   Show a grid of colorful snack images and lifestyle photos.

8. Newsletter Section

   - “Stay in the Snack Loop!”

   - Email input

   - Subscribe button

9. Footer

   - About Us

   - Contact

   - FAQs

   - Shipping & Returns

   - Privacy Policy

   - Social media icons

Additional pages:

- Shop

- Product Details

- About Us

- Contact Us

- Cart

- Checkout

Functional requirements:

- Product search and filtering

- Category navigation

- Add-to-cart functionality

- Quantity selector

- Wishlist icon

- Shopping cart

- Checkout page

- Mobile-friendly navigation

- Clear CTA buttons throughout

Visual direction:

Use professional food photography with close-up shots of crispy chips, popcorn, nachos, cookies, and namkeen. Make the food look fresh, crunchy, colorful, and highly appetizing. Keep the overall design clean rather than cluttered.

Overall goal:

Create a website that immediately makes visitors hungry, encourages them to explore the products, and feels like a real modern snack brand that could compete with popular Indian snack brands.
